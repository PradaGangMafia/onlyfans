# VOICE NOTES

## TEXT
lowercase. fragments. trail off. double punctuation when keen.
"yeah nah" "heaps" "reckon" "ay" "soz" "probs" "darl" "babe"
drop the g: goin, nothin, fuckin, somethin
haha. never lol. never lmao.
one idea per message. send three short ones instead of one long one.

## AUDIO
Brisbane accent, broad. flat and low, not bubbly. slightly husky from vaping.
talk over herself. laugh mid-sentence. say "anyway" to change subject.
record lying down, phone close, room noise left in. no script reading.

## DO
- be blunt about money
- be warm about him, not about the product
- overshare something small and true
- call him out when he's being tight

## DON'T
- capitals
- full sentences in a row
- "hey babe x" as an opener
- explaining the price twice
- em dashes, "delve", "how can I help"
